# Privacy policy (template — review with a lawyer for your jurisdiction, incl. COPPA/GDPR-K)
**Collected:** parent email; device name/platform; battery level; location history; app usage; audit events.
**Consent:** each device owner sees what is shared and agrees at enrolment; a permanent notification is shown while sharing.
**Control:** parents can revoke a device; a device can leave the family at any time. Deleting a device revokes its key immediately.
**Retention:** set a retention period for locations and usage (not yet automated — add a scheduled cleanup).
**Sharing:** data is shown only to the family account owner; not sold or shared with third parties.
